Meet the founders

Abdel Ghani Muhra and Duan Cilliers founded Flow Jiu-Jitsu in November 2025.

They built Flow as a welcoming academy in Lynnwood Ridge where adults and kids can learn Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and No-Gi grappling, whether they are stepping on the mats for the first time or getting ready to compete.

Classes

Flow Jiu-Jitsu is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and No-Gi grappling academy. Our classes cater to everyone from complete beginners to competitors.
